# Nimbus Cloud Storage — FAQ

Invented content for study purposes only (RAG pipeline test data) — not a real company or product.

## What is Nimbus Cloud Storage?

Nimbus Cloud Storage is a cloud file storage service that lets you upload, sync, and share files across devices. It offers automatic backup, file versioning, and end-to-end encryption for all paid plans.

## What plans do you offer?

We offer three plans: Free (5 GB storage, 1 device), Plus (200 GB storage, up to 5 devices, $4.99/month), and Pro (2 TB storage, unlimited devices, priority support, $12.99/month). All paid plans include a 14-day free trial.

## How do I upgrade or downgrade my plan?

Go to Account Settings > Billing > Change Plan. Upgrades take effect immediately. Downgrades take effect at the start of your next billing cycle, and you'll keep access to your current storage limit until then.

## What payment methods do you accept?

We accept all major credit cards (Visa, Mastercard, American Express), PayPal, and, for annual Pro plans only, bank transfer.

## How do I cancel my subscription?

Go to Account Settings > Billing > Cancel Subscription. Your plan remains active until the end of the current billing period. After that, your account reverts to the Free plan and any files over the 5 GB limit become read-only until you delete some or upgrade again.

## Is my data encrypted?

Yes. All files are encrypted in transit using TLS 1.3 and at rest using AES-256. Paid plans additionally support end-to-end encrypted folders, where Nimbus itself cannot read the file contents.

## Can I recover deleted files?

Deleted files go to the Trash and are recoverable for 30 days on Free and Plus plans, and 180 days on Pro. After that period, files are permanently erased and cannot be recovered.

## How do I share a file or folder with someone else?

Right-click any file or folder and select "Share." You can generate a public link, restrict access to specific email addresses, and set the link to expire after a chosen number of days. Free plan links expire automatically after 7 days.

## Does Nimbus have a mobile app?

Yes, Nimbus is available on iOS and Android. The mobile app supports automatic photo backup, offline file access for starred files, and biometric app lock.

## What happens if I exceed my storage limit?

You can still access and download existing files, but you won't be able to upload new files or sync new changes until you free up space or upgrade your plan.

## How do I contact support?

Free and Plus users can reach support via email at support@nimbus.example (invented address, for study purposes) with a typical response time of 48 hours. Pro users get priority live chat support with a typical response time of under 2 hours, available Monday to Friday, 9am to 6pm UTC.

## Can I use Nimbus for business/team accounts?

Team accounts are available as a separate "Nimbus Teams" plan, which includes shared folders, an admin console, and centralized billing. Team accounts are not covered by this FAQ.
